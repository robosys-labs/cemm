# Asset status and next production work

## Existing delivery

The original 137 individual PNGs are preserved in the complete archive. Batch 02 adds 106 standalone PNGs, 180 separate animation frames, 15 atlases and 91 editable SVG sources. The combined asset manifest addresses 438 PNGs, counting frames and atlases, but not additional previews and source boards.

Fifteen loops have twelve frames each, four-column atlases, timing and fixed pivots. Blank panels have nine-slice metadata. Road tiles cover all sixteen edge-connectivity masks. The original ZIP is preserved exactly inside the full package.

The demo is a composition preview. It supports resident selection, spiritual-overlay visibility and effect pause/play. No social outcomes, driving, mortality or spiritual transitions are computed.

## Quality boundaries

- Procedural UI and overlays are reusable prototype components; terrain is a simplified style pass.
- Existing single-pose residents are not new multidirectional character designs.
- Wingless companion states differ by tint and idle motion; they are not authored expressive action cycles.
- Halos are independent overlays rather than baked into base sprites.
- Temple radiance must read from the simulation, not the wealth of the building.
- Old panel extracts contain baked labels. Use blank components and runtime text for real interfaces.

## Acceptance

Use a consistent orthographic isometric camera, transparent backgrounds, fixed canvases and anchors, no baked labels, no clipping, reproducible naming, and genuine new directions rather than mirrored asymmetric art. Inspect at game zoom on dark and light backgrounds. Do not treat warped still images as articulated animation.
