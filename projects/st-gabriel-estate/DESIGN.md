# St Gabriel Estate design handoff

## Established direction

An isometric society simulation inspired by the depth and cause-and-effect chains of Pharaoh, not an RPG. The setting is a relatable 1990s estate: homes, roads, cars, workplaces, schools, clinics, parish life, charitable institutions and ordinary people. The player coordinates angelic companions while residents retain agency.

The central objective under discussion is sustaining and multiplying living light across generations, not maximizing a short-term religious attendance score. Exact victory thresholds remain unfinalized. Do not present the earlier 5% damnation proposal or an always-present human Beacon as settled mechanics.

## Light and companions

Light primarily resides in people. Separate golden, fading and dark halo layers convey the fictional spiritual state. Temple radiance depends on the faith and charitable life of its surrounding community, not on the cost or grandeur of the building.

Angels need not have wings. Use translucent humanoid, ribbon, wisp and energy forms. Each resident retains a companion; concerned companions continue to accompany people whose light is fading. Multiple companions can coordinate during a crisis or to prevent an accident.

Negative influences are sparse and focused around targeted situations. The distinctive deceiver has a pale-teal lollipop-like face and a dark-brown wooden stem. Difficult living conditions and reduced happiness can create vulnerability and greater support needs; they must not automatically imply guilt, demonic possession or a dark halo.

## Persistent society

People, relationships, households, institutions and events share one calendar and finite time. Track immediate decisions, repeated habits and long-term consequences. Parents, marriages, children, newcomers, essential welfare, people requesting help, living standards, charitable response and institutional reliability are central.

Faith is explicit. Prayer, novenas, crusades, celebrations, teaching, sacraments, repentance, parish organization, priest health and pastoral workload require real schedules, accessible participation and follow-through. A religious event is not an instant universal stat boost.

Traffic safety uses ordinary vehicle and pedestrian situations, supported by coordinated guidance and practical prevention. The prototype has visual intervention assets; it does not yet compute driving or accident outcomes.

## Selected resident panel

Show age, happiness, total charity given in a special gameplay value, grace percentage, faith, halo/light level, people helped or brought toward light, estimated destiny fulfillment, estimated remaining life, essential needs, relationships, assignments, influences and causal history. Values are fictional game abstractions, not theological judgments or medical estimates.

## Next useful vertical slice

A persistent estate district with several households, one parish and priest, employment and welfare dependencies, a newcomer arrival, a novena, a celebration, and a traffic hazard. A useful result must show traceable consequences and meaningful differences between money, time, care, prayer, teaching, truth, accountability and reconciliation.

The current delivery is an asset library and composition preview. It is not the completed vertical slice.
