# Asset status and next production work

## Completed in this batch

Original assets are preserved byte-for-byte. New PNGs are individually addressable; SVG variants are separately editable. Fifteen loops have twelve separate frames each, a four-column atlas, timing and fixed pivots. Blank panels are text-free and include nine-slice metadata. Road tiles cover all sixteen edge-connectivity masks.

The demo is a composition preview. It supports resident selection, spiritual-overlay visibility and effect pause/play. Its buttons do not constitute a simulation engine; no social outcomes, actual driving, mortality or spiritual transitions are computed.

## Quality boundaries

- The procedural UI and overlays are reusable prototype components; terrain is a simplified style pass.
- Existing single-pose characters are not new character designs in this update.
- Wingless companion states differ by tint and idle motion; no new sad/happy facial expressions were fabricated.
- Strong, weak and dark halos are independent overlay assets. They must not be baked into resident base sprites.
- Temple radiance must be attached to a simulation-owned aura value, not to the wealth of the building.
- Several old source panels include baked-in example labels. Use the editable demo UI and blank components instead when building real interfaces.

## Acceptance for future sprite sets

Consistent orthographic isometric camera; transparent background; no baked labels or presentation frames; no clipping; reproducible naming; a fixed canvas and foot anchor; genuine new directions rather than mirrored asymmetric art; meaningful poses rather than merely warped still images. Inspect at normal game zoom on both dark and light backgrounds.

## Theology and simulation boundaries

The prototype is not an assessment of real holiness, grace or salvation. An unhappy resident may retain a bright halo; a comfortable resident may repeatedly choose harmful conduct. Do not automatically convert material hardship to darkness, demon possession or moral guilt. The stated design uses sparse targeted temptation and persistent compassionate guardians.
