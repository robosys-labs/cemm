# G3 physical receipt preflight for G5

This TRAIN-only preflight measures the observable interface between the frozen G3 world and a future answer-retention study. Its inputs are the 16 published v3 teaching episodes and matching NAND worlds for seeds 7, 11, 23, 37 and 53. Use the same pinned training manifest, teaching artifact and lexical registry as the coordinate intervention at `5fbcaee`. The five worlds share those 16 teaching observations.

For each episode, reuse `effect_role_model.pack` to compute the four candidate actions and their full endpoint distributions from the observed physical context. Preserve the native hidden-mode marginalization. Record the physical action tuple with each distribution, the episode and evidence IDs, and a digest of the public prefix containing entities, anchor, delivered context and current utterance text. Record the observed endpoint only after the forecasts. Candidate indices remain local to that event. No across-layout or across-identity alignment is assumed.

Report all six pairwise total-variation distances, the four likelihoods of the observed endpoint, and their normalized uniform-prior diagnostic posterior. Mark distribution pairs within `1e-10` total variation as numerically indistinguishable. This tolerance checks equality at the numerical interface. It does not establish a scientific confidence cutoff or determine which observations to include. Retain every row and every ambiguous pair.

Verify that changing each current endpoint to its bitwise complement changes neither the prefix digest nor the prior hidden-mode state, language features, action tuples or candidate likelihoods. Require exactly one utterance per episode, all 16 source IDs once per seed, finite normalized candidate distributions, valid observed-endpoint probabilities and exact reproducibility on a second invocation. Report a failure at the earliest violated invariant. The output records per-seed and per-row measurements without fitting parameters, choosing queries, reopening the challenge, or changing a prior admission decision.

Completion establishes a traceable physical-answer interface and its local ambiguity. Existing word identities, layouts and per-word coordinates bound the evidence. A later G5 study must separately measure learned retention through restart and transfer on fresh instances with matched histories and goals.
