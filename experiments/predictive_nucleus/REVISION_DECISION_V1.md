## Material result and limits

An earlier contradiction is *not* sufficient to commit a new meaning.
The bounded memory keeps both alternatives until another distinct physical
receipt distinguishes a revision from a one-time exception; non-distinguishing
payoffs leave it unresolved. Scope-specific updates do not rewrite a parent
or sibling, and reset preserves a second independently learned word.
A most-specific/latest-only rule matches 91.67% unassisted accuracy, but
confidently commits in the unresolved stratum and cannot buy beneficial
clarification. This new policy uses **no trainable parameters** and no
additional G3/G4 model capacity.

The two interpretations, scope hierarchy, provenance/verification flag,
conditional G3 values, and perfect/available query are supplied. Memory
retention is simulated by an in-process snapshot, not tested across a
process crash or a production data store. The inherited physical challenge
bank was opened during G3/G4. The 92,160 rows reuse a small set of physical
menus and the same structured task across five seeds: they are not 92,160
independent environments. No raw held-out decision bank was published before
scoring; the code and registered gates were frozen beforehand. This is
**development evidence for a baseline, not formal G5 admission or independent
scientific replication**.

## Next high-information experiment

Fit a shared update policy *from selected action outcomes* on otherwise
identical histories where the physical truth of a local exception or durable
revision differs. Withhold whole scope/word/trajectory families, keep a
similarly informed dated-evidence baseline and the original protected
G3 history. Score calibrated ambiguity, evidence duplication, old meanings,
number of clarifications and cost. Require superiority or exact simplicity
parity under matched data/compute before increasing capacity. Avoid another
new framework or shadow runtime. G6–G8 remain open.
