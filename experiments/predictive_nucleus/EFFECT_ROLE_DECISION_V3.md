## What changed and what was learned

The v3 candidate preserves the entire accepted 88-scalar coordinate language
parent. It adds four trainable scalars: one effect-family coordinate and one
role coordinate for each of two exact new lexical identities. All four start
at zero, giving a uniform initial distribution over the four supplied native
actions. Effect-family probability is separated from layout-conditioned role
confidence. Grounded fitting uses observed endpoints; it receives no gold
participant order or action label. Only the parser control receives action
supervision.

All ten acquired native worlds remain frozen at 562 parameters. Five language
seeds are fitted on their matching NAND world, then each fixed language model
is evaluated with both NAND and NOR worlds. Every arm uses the unchanged
16-event teaching tape, 1,152-event rehearsal tape, 256-update recipe and
predeclared numerical criteria. There are 15 fits: grounded, parser and text-only
for seeds 7, 11, 23, 37 and 53. Grounded/parser models contain 92 scalars each,
of which only four train; text-only has 1,048 trainable parameters.

Legacy identities retain the frozen parent's contact-action support by
construction. This protects established meanings and prevents the new effect
from consuming their probability mass. It is **structural retention**, with
additional storage per new identity. It does not demonstrate shared-parameter
continual learning, changing senses of old words, unrestricted polysemy or
discovery of the supplied action menu. Unknown raw identities remain separately
unresolved.

retained exact worlds remain correct, with zero exact-world regressions, zero
changed world MAPs and zero changed action MAPs. The largest bank mean NLL is

## Preserved history and next boundary

The completed v1 study remains **REVISE_EFFECT_ROLE_GROUNDING**, including its
original probability failures and exact-world/action regressions. Original v2
remains **STOP_INCOMPLETE_EVIDENCE**: its published weights survived, but its
original raw collection was lost before publication and was never scored.
The v2 reference forecasts in this study are new AMD-runtime evidence and do not
retroactively complete or rewrite v2. Accepted G1/G2 development foundations,
coordinate transfer and native SET v2 acquisition remain intact.

The next permitted step is a small G4 tranche: freeze the accepted G3 models
and study two agents with different observations, one bounded hidden-state
information gap and external goals.
Messages must remain observed, source-attributed acts; no supplied
ASK/ASSERT/COMMAND labels or automatic admission of untrusted utterances. Freeze
held-out truthful/misleading messages, useful versus useless questions, and
matched silent/random/shuffled-message controls before training. The decision
must depend on improved held-out utility and the causal effect of delivered
information at equal query/step budgets. Broader source conflict, delayed
evidence and context-change requirements remain part of G4's eventual gate.

This result does not unlock corpus ingestion or the Wikipedia 3× claim. General
calibration, independent custody and matched consumed compute remain open. The
