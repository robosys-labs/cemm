## Smallest added capability

A 474-parameter character-feature GRU learns which of two identity-mention
permutations an utterance denotes. Its probability distribution enters the same
562-parameter acquired world, which predicts a complete joint world. Training
uses observed consequences, with no gold action order, role label, language ID
or parser program in the candidate. The implementation marginalizes uncertainty
over operand order while retaining the physical-mode/world mixture. Full feedback
updates the physical-mode belief; it does not update encoder weights or the
encoder's order distribution during evaluation.

One adapter per seed is trained on NAND outcomes and applied unchanged to both
the acquired NAND and acquired NOR checkpoints. This tests reuse of the binding,
not acquisition of a new physical law from language. The separately acquired
worlds remain separate; there is no unified-law claim.

**Supplied structure:** opaque physical identity handles, exact name equality,
whitespace token boundaries, one contact event per three-token utterance, two
candidate operand orders, the existing two-mode world hypothesis and mixture
arithmetic. The event kind is fixed to contact. The language choice and order
are learned. Synonyms appear during fitting, while their alternating sequences
are held out. Unknown synonym semantics and arbitrary sentences remain untested.

## Frozen prospective design

| Component | Prescribed design |
|---|---|
| Languages | English-like SVO; artificial OSV with distinct lexical forms |
| Training | 256 episodes / 384 text forecasts; NAND only; one/two contacts; 3/4 entities |
| Challenge per law | 576 episodes / 2,080 forecasts; new names; chain/fork/cycle/repeated/disjoint families; 3–5 contacts; 3–6 entities |
| Language seeds | 7, 11, 23, 37, 53 |
| Training budget | 256 full-data Adam updates per arm/seed; learning rate .02; gradient clipping 5 |
| World fitting | None; ten acquired checkpoints retain their exact bytes |
| Fitted arms | Outcome-grounded; supervised parser; direct neural sequence; text-only; random frozen-world grounding ablation |
| Evaluation ablation | Exchange the two names in each message while preserving observed feedback |
| Complete denominator | 60 traces × 2,080 = 124,800 forecasts; evaluator-native references separate |

The supervised parser is a control with privileged order labels. The direct
sequence model receives the same sufficient observed context and current text,
without acquired world weights. It has 1,048 parameters against 1,036 total for
the grounded candidate. New language exposures and updates match, but previous
world-learning exposure and consumed compute do not. In addition, 5/6-entity
transfer exposes untrained output coordinates in the direct control; its results
at the trained entity counts, 3/4, must be reported separately. These are diagnostic
controls, not a strong architecture-superiority experiment.

All bilingual/synonym twins remain in the same split. Physical modes, reversals,
languages, laws and seeds are dependent views. The bank is publicly generated by
the same research author; it is not independently sealed.

## Decisions fixed before fitting

The [versioned protocol](bilingual_protocol_v1.json) fixes the complete criteria:
at least 95% exact-world/order accuracy in every law/seed/family/language cell,
mean NLL at most .05, critical true-world probability at least .9 and reversed
counterfactual probability at most .1, twin TV at most .02, and zero exact-world
regressions against the native gold-action reference. Both-direction whole-
trajectory conjunctions must pass. The candidate must show the declared
message/grounding dependence, and remain within .02 accuracy of the parser.
The sequence comparison is reported without an architecture-superiority gate.

Every failed case, native probability loss and control result remains in the
denominator. A completed failure yields `REVISE_BILINGUAL_GROUNDING`; malformed
or incomplete execution yields `STOP_INCOMPLETE_EXECUTION`. A successful bounded
result yields `KEEP_BILINGUAL_GROUNDING`. None changes scientific admission.

Use the exact pinned Python 3.13.16 / Torch 2.10.0+cpu / NumPy 2.3.5 environment,
one numerical thread and deterministic algorithms. The runner checks the
environment and clean committed source. Output directories are exclusive, and
failed attempts are retained. Cached forecast timing is reported separately from
native likelihood/token preparation; batch-amortized latency is not individual
request latency. The full incremental source/test count and retained research
count accompany the result; the inclusive 2,000-line objective remains unmet.
