## Smallest changed learner

All 88 coordinate-parent scalars remain frozen. Each of two newly registered
identities gets an effect logit `e` and role coordinate `r`, both initially zero.
The new role coordinate combines with the inherited learned layout coordinates
through the parent's bilinear readout, with its common offset removed. Normalize
the two role scores separately from the two effect-family scores `[0, e]`; their
product gives probabilities over the four supplied actions. Neutral coordinates
initially assign one quarter to every action. Endpoints determine the learned
sign, magnitude and effect preference; no gold action or role label enters the
grounded learner. Only the supervised parser control reads action labels.

Legacy identities retain the parent's two-contact-action support with zero SET
mass. That restriction is a supplied architectural choice. It protects retention
structurally, so success would not show that shared trainable parameters learned
to avoid forgetting. Legacy/new membership records acquisition provenance;
it does not assign a gold meaning to either new identity. Storage grows by two
scalars per added exact identity. This study makes no shared-parameter lexical
acquisition, zero-shot meaning or unrestricted polysemy claim.

All ten previously acquired native worlds remain frozen at 562 parameters each.
They were learned from nonverbal experience before language fitting. Each NAND
world supplies its matching seed's language endpoint likelihoods; the same
fitted language state is then used with both acquired NAND and NOR worlds.

## Frozen decision

Every original v1/v2 numerical criterion is preserved. Every law/seed and
applicable family, layout, word-age and effect stratum must meet the original
identifiable-world, action, conditional-KL, ambiguity, trajectory, paired-role,
bilingual-TV and goal gates. The minimum effect-sensitive gain over masked and
text-only controls remains .10. No unsupported MAP world or known fixed-coordinate
error is allowed. All six historical banks require zero exact-world regressions,
unchanged MAP action, and mean truth NLL at most .05; new SET mass is not removed
by renormalization.

Both opened challenge banks must also pass all unchanged candidate gates with
current controls. Canonical forecasts must cause zero formerly correct
identifiable-world regressions and zero formerly correct globally taught-action
regressions, against archived v1 or the frozen-v2 current-runtime reference as
appropriate. Ambiguous actual-world changes and masked repairs/regressions
remain fully reported descriptively. No failed condition may be omitted.

Incomplete evidence, wrong runtime, malformed provenance, mutation, orphan or
duplicate shard artifacts, missing rows or a failed audit stop progression. A
complete valid run that fails any criterion yields `REVISE_EFFECT_ROLE_GROUNDING`.
Only every prospective criterion and identity control passing permits
`DEVELOPMENT_G3_COMPLETE` and the next development stage G4. Formal scientific
admission remains false.
