# G3 effect, role and goal transfer — prospective v1

This study closes the remaining **development** G3 requirements only if every
predeclared check passes. It starts from accepted native SET acquisition v2 and
the accepted coordinate language model. It does not refit G1/G2, alter a prior
decision, or claim formal scientific admission.

## Smallest model change

The world retains all 562 parameters. The language encoder retains its 81 lexical
and three layout parameters and extends its bilinear output from two to four
alternatives, for **92 parameters** total. All 88 inherited values are copied;
four added values start at zero. The action alternatives are word-independent:
contact in either direction, or set either mentioned participant to one.

Action probabilities are marginalized together with the same two persistent
physical modes. Each observed endpoint is delivered only after its forecast.
The new words have no parser labels in the grounded arm. Across contrasting
teaching situations their native effect is identifiable, even though each
individual endpoint admits more than one native action.

The exact-string lexical registry adds supplied identity features, not meanings.
It preserves existing character features unless a new token collides, in which
case that new token receives deterministic distinct features. Unknown tokens
remain distinct typed unresolved identities. Neither POS nor a permanent single
sense is assigned by the registry.

## Fixed exposure and challenge

Each of five seeds trains one grounded encoder, one supervised parser and one
text-only control. Every fit has 256 Adam updates at learning rate .02, clip norm
5, fresh optimizer state and a 400-second CPU ceiling. Half the objective weight
is the mean over 16 new teaching observations; half is the mean over the exact
1,152-event historical TRAIN tape. There is no loss-based selection or retry.

Each law contributes 1,536 fresh episodes and 6,912 forecast events: 6,144 have
singleton conditional support and 768 remain ambiguous. The 1,152 newword/VOS
events are absent from new-word teaching. Earlier feedback is observed, so whole
trajectory metrics do not measure autonomous rollouts.

There are 384 groups of four final hypothetical alternatives per law. Every
group shares the exact same observed prefix; alternatives never receive one
another's hypothetical outcomes. Its four goals fix the mentioned two bits and
leave every spectator unchanged. The set includes unattainable goals. The
NAND/NOR inventories contain respectively 558/714 effect-sensitive goals,
including 186/294 SET-required and 372/420 contact-required goals. These are
dependent views of controlled situations, not independent experimental trials.

Fresh collection saves grounded canonical and masked forecasts, parser forecasts
and text-only forecasts: 276,480 rows in total. The six historical banks add
131,520 grounded rows. Supplied goal candidates and targets are evaluated from
these saved distributions; the study adds no natural-language goal parser.

## Decision and failure handling

The JSON protocol is authoritative for numerical criteria. Every seed and both
laws must pass; aggregate success cannot conceal a failed stratum. In particular,
zero unsupported MAP predictions makes singleton support exact despite a .95
accuracy floor. Conditional KL, ambiguity TV, trajectories, reversals, learned
actions, aligned bilingual forecasts, goal accuracy and regret all have explicit
bounds. Effect-sensitive goal choices must improve by at least .1 over both
masked and text-only controls.

Retention uses the accepted native SET v2 language forecasts on the identical
world checkpoints. It requires zero exact-world regressions, mean NLL at most
.05, and unchanged old action decisions after adding SET alternatives. Every
probability drift, adverse NLL delta and new SET mass remains visible.

Invalid evidence or provenance aborts. Valid scientific failures finish every
report and yield `REVISE_EFFECT_ROLE_GROUNDING`. Only a complete pass yields
`DEVELOPMENT_G3_COMPLETE` and development readiness for G4. The separate original
scientific gate remains `NOT_ADMITTED`; same-team hashes/review do not establish
independent custody. Parameter and consumed-compute matching are not claimed.
The inclusive 2,000-line research goal remains unmet.
