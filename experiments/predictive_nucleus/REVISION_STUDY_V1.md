# G5 scoped meaning correction — prospective bounded protocol v1

**Parent:** PR #85 at `f6a9ce6f531375079fff9ed4761f81cbaaf28bb3`.
**Claim:** development of durable evidence-grounded correction and uncertainty,
not neural discovery of a general language, an admitted G5 milestone, or an
independently authored blind world. Existing physical challenge traces have
already been opened in earlier G3/G4 studies. The *scope/revision task* is new.

## Fixed task and held-out selection

Do not modify any inherited 562-parameter world or 92-scalar language
checkpoint, source bank, historical protocol or existing score. Select exactly
six triples of sites per law and seed from frozen `transfer_task.catalogue`
with split `challenge`, based on **metadata only** (distinct lineage, roster,
three word orders). All five inherited seeds (7, 11, 23, 37, 53) and both laws
(NAND/NOR) are compulsory. All candidate physical action probabilities and
payoffs come from the frozen G3-derived catalogue.

Use two terms: `qor` may change meaning, `nav` is an independent retained
sentinel. Interpretations 0/1 are supplied candidates with conditional G3
forecasts. The learner receives no meaning label. Physical evidence is an
observed selected-action payoff and both candidate likelihoods. The scope
hierarchy and source/time/evidence IDs are supplied.

At the root, physically establish `qor` and `nav`. At a child scope,
establish a potentially different `qor` meaning through two distinct physical
receipts. At a unit scope, introduce one contradictory physical receipt. The
contradiction may represent either a lasting revision or an exception; until
the later probe's selected payoff arrives, the two target worlds are
indistinguishable. The later probe is either discriminating, or observationally
identical under both possible interpretations. Deliver an unverified assertion
and an optionally duplicated stale root receipt afterwards. Clear all episode
memory; only serialized evidence carries to a new site/roster/layout. Query
three destinations: the revised unit, a sibling inheriting its region, and an
outside unit inheriting root.

## Memory mechanism and baselines

`revision_memory.py` stores one receipt per **physical evidence identity**.
The first conflicting physical record records a rival hypothesis, not a new
certainty. Another distinguishable physical outcome can confirm or refute it.
A verified record whose candidate likelihoods are indistinguishable does not
manufacture certainty. Contradictory independent records at the same time
remain unresolved. Unverified claims never become physical truth. A later
duplicate does not become new evidence. Only supplied nested scopes propagate.

Compare with a no-memory questioner and a strongest simple, most-specific
latest-receipt baseline. The query is **supplied**, optional, perfect and costs
0.05 or 0.75, or is unavailable. Some unresolved cases have no distinguishing
query at all. Any correct reply is evaluator-only and is supplied to the
candidate only after it requests clarification. Report goal correctness and
question cost separately, not pooled misleading scores.

## Immutable registered gates

Within each of ten law/seed conditions: 9,216 target contexts, 7,680 resolved
and 1,536 genuinely unresolved, with both latent outcomes equally represented
under identical public information. For the 7,680 resolved cases require
100% prequestion correctness, zero repeat questions, and 100% retention of the
independent sentinel and parent scopes. For unresolved cases require an
explicit `None` (not a confident guess), exactly 50% best possible
prequestion correctness, exactly 768 cheap informative clarifications and no
costly or unavailable queries; uninformative queries must not be purchased.
No memory reset or duplicate may change the corresponding choices.

At the cheapest price require higher net reward than the no-memory policy
and fewer questions; report counts of all misses and cases where the
latest-receipt baseline is confidently wrong. Require tests for conflicting
receipt IDs, contradictory same-time physical observations and verification
versus assertion. Do not change thresholds after any test outcome is opened.
Store the original failure and revise under a new challenge if a gate fails.

No trainable scalar is added; the **retained state comes from experience**,
using a supplied two-hypothesis belief updater. A pass demonstrates a bounded
durable evidence memory, not independently learned scope logic or unrestricted
continual learning. Record that distinction and keep formal G5 open.
